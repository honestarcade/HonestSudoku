# Honest Sudoku — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-09-19** · Last updated: 2026-09-19

Honest Sudoku (package `com.honestarcade.sudoku`), published by Honest Arcade,
collects **no data**. None.

- The app has **no network access**. The Android package requests no
  permissions at all — the INTERNET permission included — so it is technically
  incapable of sending anything anywhere. This is enforced by an automated test
  and by a scan of every built bundle, and you can verify it yourself: the app
  is open source at <https://github.com/honestarcade/HonestSudoku>.
- No personal information, identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports,
  advertising IDs, or diagnostics are collected, stored, shared, or sold — by
  us or by any third party. The app is built with Flutter and contains no
  advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind, and
  no plugin with network access. Nothing in the app can transmit data
  regardless, because it holds no INTERNET permission.
- Your puzzle in progress, your statistics (solves, streaks, best and average
  times) and your settings are saved **only on your device** and never leave
  it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- The app shows no ads and contains no purchases.
- Children's privacy: since no data is collected from anyone, no data is
  collected from children.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for you to request a copy of or
ask us to delete — but questions are always welcome.
